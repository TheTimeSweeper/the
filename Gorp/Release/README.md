# Gorp
I just needed a break from all the mod updating

Adds a new teleporter boss, Gorp, to all maps that have Gups
- fully multiplayer compatible
- decently configurable
- boss item coming soon maybe

Feedback absolutely welcome! Reach out to me (`thetimesweeper`) on the ror2 modding discord or the enforcer discord (https://discord.gg/GgjjDvStcV) and let me know what you think!

___ 
## What it do
- Passive: gets smaller and faster as it loses health
- Passive: absorbs nearby gips geeps gups and gorps to heal and get larger
- Attack1: gup spike attack but with an actual telegraph and higher damage
- Attack2: spends its health to mortar shoot three gips at you, predicts your movement
